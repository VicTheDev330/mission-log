# IKEA Campaign Initiative

## 1. Campaign

**Brand:** IKEA

**Campaign Name:** IKEA: Affordable Furniture for Better Everyday Living

---

## 2. Campaign Idea

Make everyday living better with affordable, functional and stylish home solutions from IKEA.

---

## 3. Target Audience

Young adults, students and families in India who want affordable, practical and attractive solutions for organizing and improving their homes.

---

## 4. Customer Benefits

- "I can make my home look better without spending too much."
- "I can find smart solutions for small spaces."
- "I can choose practical furniture that fits my everyday needs."

|---|---|---|---|

| 8 | IKEA Stores | Physical | Product experience and purchases |
| 9 | Google Maps | Local/Digital | Store discovery |
| 10 | Online Advertising | Advertising | Brand promotion |
| 11 | Product Packaging | Physical | Product information and brand experience |
| 12 | Customer Support | Service | Customer assistance |

---

## 8. Key Findings

### Most Surprising Touchpoint

IKEA's physical stores are a surprising touchpoint because they provide more than a place to purchase products. Customers can explore room setups, experience products and get ideas for improving their homes.

### Most Neglected Touchpoint

Email communication appears less prominent compared with IKEA's website, social media channels and physical stores.
