## 2. DoomCopy Threat Analysis

DoomCopy could copy public product descriptions, advertisements, social posts, FAQs and promotional messages. It could use copied content to impersonate the brand or mislead customers.

It cannot automatically copy private CRM records, genuine customer consent, verified internal analytics or trusted customer relationships simply from public content. We should protect customer data, verify official links and use appropriate consent controls.

## 3. Objectives

### North Star
Increase qualified engagement with IKEA's affordable everyday-living campaign and encourage more customers to explore relevant products.

### SMART KPIs — 100-Day Targets

1. **Website traffic:** Increase campaign-attributed visits by 20% against the first 14 days of measured baseline traffic.
2. **Engagement:** Increase engaged sessions from campaign traffic by 15% against the first 14 days of baseline data.
3. **Product consideration:** Increase product-detail views per campaign-attributed session by 10% against the first 14 days of baseline data.

*These are proposed targets, not achieved results. Measure them using available GA4 practice data if actual IKEA analytics are unavailable.*
